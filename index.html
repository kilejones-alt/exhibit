The Third Era is a groundbreaking historical exhibition exploring the evolution of Jew-hatred and the emergence of antizionism through archival documents, primary sources, historical imagery, multimedia, and personal testimony.

Developed under the scholarly leadership of Dr. Naya Lekht, a leading scholar and educator on antizionism and creator of the Three Eras of Jew-Hatred framework, the exhibition will examine how centuries-old anti-Jewish libels were reinvented and transformed into modern antizionism.

THE THREE ERAS FRAMEWORK

Era 1 — Religious (Anti-Judaism)
Religious Jew-hatred rooted in antiquity and perpetuated across pre-modern Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa (MENA).
Era 2 — Racial (Antisemitism)
Racial Jew-hatred that emerged as a modern ideology in the 19th century, with the term antisemitism popularized in 1879, culminating in the Holocaust.
Era 3 — National (Antizionism)
The reinvention of centuries-old anti-Jewish libels targeting the Jewish collective — its people, national identity, and state.
